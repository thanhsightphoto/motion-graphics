# Instagram caption

Short, skimmable on a phone, built around the video's rules (the same ones on the cover and the summary clip).

Structure:
1. Hook line: the shot + the tension, one emoji at most.
2. One line setting up the "read it first" idea.
3. One numbered block per rule: situation → paddle face → swing path → why (the coach's reason, in plain words).
4. One 💡 line with the body-mechanics cue, if the video has one.
5. The coach's closing insight, restated.
6. Call to action (save / share / tag a partner).
7. 6–10 hashtags: broad (#pickleball) + the shot (#twoey #dink) + intent (#pickleballtips #pickleballdrills).

Rules: every claim must come from the video or the user's framing (no invented stats or promises); if you add a general
technique cue (e.g. "soft hands, absorb it"), tell the user it's yours so they can swap in their own. Keep it under ~120
words before hashtags; English unless asked otherwise. Offer a Vietnamese version or a one-line Reels version as an option, don't write them unasked.

Example 1 (twoey dink, 2026-09-27):

```
Twoey dink: same shot, two different balls 🏓

Before you lift, read the spin coming at you:

1️⃣ No-spin dink → close the paddle face slightly and swing more forward. Nothing on the ball to fight, so drive it through with a bit more pace.

2️⃣ Heavy slice (backspin + sidespin) → open the paddle face and swing straight up, low to high, with very little forward. Swing forward against that spin and it goes into the net.

💡 Use your hips to lift, not your arm, and keep the face square to the ball so the sidespin doesn't pull it down.

Your technique isn't the problem. Reading the ball is. 👀

Save this for your next dink session 🔖

#pickleball #twoey #dink #pickleballtips #pickleballdrills #pickleballcoach #dinking #topspin #pickleballlife
```

Example 2 (reset & counter, 2026-10-03; the user is the player, so it's written in first person for the rule):

```
Reset & Counter: two answers to a speed-up 🏓

Most players counter every speed-up at the kitchen. A blind counter goes into the net or pops up and gets put away.

Read it first:

1️⃣ No read → block & reset. Paddle out front, soft hands, absorb it, and drop it back into their kitchen. You're neutral again.

2️⃣ Good read → counter. If you see it coming, go for it and send it back at their feet.

Off-speed attacks too: still a speed-up, same read.

My rule: block, block… until I get a good read. Then I go. 👀

Save this for your next kitchen session 🔖

#pickleball #pickleballtips #pickleballdrills #resetandcounter #kitchendefense #speedup #counterattack #pickleballlife
```
